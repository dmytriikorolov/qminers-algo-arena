# Baseline cleanup and targeted experiments

The supplied baseline reproduced 71,080 on round3_public and 158,426 on
round2_public with the official defaults: 5 ms latency, 100-lot inventory
limit, and 10,000 per second breach penalty. The reported 170k result has
not been reproduced; its dataset/settings are still unknown.

## Changes

- baseline.py: 508 to 389 lines. Removed inactive bandit, Kelly, Thompson,
  jump-context and associated accounting/configuration; replaced stale notes.
  Every SimulationResult field matches the original round 3 replay exactly.
- baseline_candidate.py: standalone cleaned baseline with forecast sizing
  thresholds changed from 1.4/2.2 to 1.6/2.6. No model weights were fitted.
- scripts/tune_baseline.py: seven predefined parameter/mechanics experiments.
- original.py in this directory preserves the input file before cleanup.

## Measured results

| Round 3 variant | Net PnL | First half | Second half | Max drawdown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Shorter shock pause | 70,210 | 38,724 | 31,486 | 2,336 |
| Longer shock pause | 70,959 | 38,217 | 32,742 | 2,336 |
| No wide-spread improvement | 52,663 | 30,826 | 21,837 | 12,661 |
| No flip allowance | 50,340 | 29,599 | 20,741 | 2,353 |
| Larger quote risk horizon | 63,703 | 34,329 | 29,374 | 2,231 |
| Sizing thresholds 1.6/2.6 | 82,067 | 43,486 | 38,581 | 3,763 |
| Strict pending-cancel exposure | 66,292 | 36,167 | 30,125 | 2,336 |

The original/cleaned round 3 baseline earns 71,080. All variants except strict
exposure incur a 50-point breach penalty; strict exposure incurs zero.

The standalone candidate was replayed separately: 82,067 on round 3 (+15.5%)
and 166,022 on round 2 (+4.8% versus 158,426). The candidate accepts more
inventory risk during moderate forecast volatility. It improves both round 3
halves but increases drawdown. These are public-data selection results;
neither half is an untouched holdout, and round 2 trained the ridge weights.

## Recommendation

Keep the wide-spread flattening improvement and partial flip allowance.
Changing shock duration alone has little benefit. The less conservative sizing
ramp is the strongest tested candidate. The next substantive extension is a
quote-specific fill/markout model, with labels generated by actual simulator
fills and validation on fresh data. Preserve explicit pending-order exposure
tracking; stricter accounting removes the observed penalty at a PnL cost.

## Reproduce

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m simulator.main --strategy strategies/baseline_candidate.py --backtest data/round3_public.csv --log-dir logs/baseline_candidate_check --no-logs
.venv/bin/python scripts/tune_baseline.py higher_limit_threshold
```
